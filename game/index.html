A card duel in the Ashen Court

EMBERFALL

Vesk, the Ashen Warlock, holds the court. You hold twenty-four cards, three energy a turn, and a sword made of embers. Read his intent. Chain your suits. Bring him down.

The duel. Both duelists have 30 life. Each turn you draw 2 cards and refill to 3 energy. Click a card (or press 1–8) to play it; Space ends your turn.

Four suits. Ember deals damage, Aegis raises block, Thorn stacks poison that ticks past block at the start of a turn, Tide draws cards and heals.

Resonance. Play two cards of the same suit in a row and the second resonates: +1 to its damage, block, poison and healing.

Intent. Above Vesk's portrait, his intent shows the biggest thing his hand can do next turn. Wall up against a sword. Race him when he schemes.

An original card game · no two duels deal alike
